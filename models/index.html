How the free portfolio shoots work.

HillJackz Productions is booking 5 free portfolio shoots in the Columbus, OH area. You get a free photoshoot plus edited digital images for your portfolio. All looks and all experience levels are welcome. Must be 18+. Model release + portfolio permission required.

You choose your own looks and wardrobe. We'll agree on the style and comfort level in writing before the shoot, and you can stop anytime.

Trade terms

This is an unpaid trade collaboration: you get edited digital images for your portfolio, and in exchange you sign our portfolio permission so HillJackz Productions can show selected images in our portfolio, website, and social media.
