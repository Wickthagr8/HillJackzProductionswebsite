About HillJackz Productions

Creative work with a local point of view.

HillJackz Productions is an independent creative production company based in the Columbus, Ohio area. We help people and small businesses share what makes them worth noticing through video, sound, and photography.

Our story

Built around making things that connect.

HillJackz Productions grew from hands-on creative projects in video, audio, and original storytelling. The goal is to bring that same personal, practical approach to local businesses: understand what you want people to remember, then make a clear piece of media around it.

We keep projects focused and collaborative. Before production starts, we agree on the scope, schedule, price, and final files so you know what to expect.

What we do

One creative home for your message.

Video

Digital, live-action, and spokesperson commercials for small businesses.

Audio

Spoken audio ads and original jingles shaped around your message.

Photography

Business photos, portraits, professional headshots, and private modeling portfolio sessions.

Aerial images
